Motion
Camera
Turn swings the flag away from you around its pole. Tilt leans it back or forward. Perspective makes the far end smaller. Zoom only changes the preview, not exports.
Light and edges
Fold shading brightens and darkens the cloth where it folds. Sheen adds a highlight on folds that face the light. Alpha cutoff hides pixels that are more see-through than the value, which trims soft edges.
